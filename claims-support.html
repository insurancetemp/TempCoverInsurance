Claims information

Will a temporary policy affect my No Claims Bonus?
Tempcover's claims guidance explains that temporary insurance does not build or use your No Claims Bonus, and it should not affect the bonus on your separate annual policy.
Where can I find my policy excess?
Check your Policy Schedule, which is included with the policy documents issued when you bought the cover. You can also read Tempcover's guide to understanding car insurance excess.
Am I entitled to a courtesy vehicle?
This depends on the cover provided by your policy. Check your Policy Wording and Insurance Product Information to see whether a courtesy vehicle is included and what conditions apply.
How do I make a claim?
If your insured vehicle is involved in an accident or another incident, contact the claims team using the number on your Certificate of Motor Insurance or policy wording. Call as soon as you reasonably can, particularly if the vehicle needs recovering.
What should I do immediately after an accident?
  • Stop at the scene. Leaving without stopping may be an offence.
  • Switch on your hazard warning lights.
  • Check whether anyone is hurt or in danger. Call the emergency services if needed.
  • Exchange the relevant details with the other people involved.
  • Photograph the damage, vehicle registrations, where the vehicles came to rest, and any relevant road signs or markings.
  • Do not admit fault or apologise in a way that could be understood as accepting responsibility.
  • Collect contact details from any witnesses.
Contact the claims team promptly, especially if recovery is required.
I have RAC Breakdown Cover. Who do I call?
If RAC Breakdown Cover is included with your purchase, use the contact route in your RAC breakdown documents or visit the RAC website to access its breakdown assistance service.
Can I claim for a damaged or broken windscreen?
Check your own policy documents before arranging repairs or making a claim. They explain whether windscreen damage is covered and any applicable limits or excess.
