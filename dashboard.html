Help & Support

Information and ways to contact us

How can we help?

Find answers to common account questions or use the contact details below to get in touch with the relevant team.

Frequently asked questions
How do I view my insurance certificate?
Return to Home and select “View insurance certificate” on your policy card. Your certificate opens on a separate page. If your certificate is unavailable, contact support.
How can I check when my cover expires?
Your Home screen shows the remaining time on your policy. You can also open Policy Details to review the recorded start and expiry dates.
Why can't I see my policy?
Check that you are signed in to the account used for your policy. If your policy is missing or the information looks incorrect, contact support so your details can be checked.
How do I check or correct my personal details?
Open Profile from the bottom navigation to review the information held in your account. Contact support if anything needs correcting.
What should I do if I have an accident?
Follow the instructions in your policy documents and contact the relevant claims team using the details provided below. In an emergency, contact the emergency services.
How do I make a complaint?
You can contact the complaints team at complaints@tempcover.com. Include your name, policy number if available, and a clear explanation of your concern.
