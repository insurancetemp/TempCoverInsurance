Who do we cover?

We can cover you if you’re aged between 17 and 77 years old and hold a full UK or EU driving licence.

Your car must also be taxed, roadworthy and valued between £1,500 and £65,000, alongside some other eligibility requirements.

From quick trips to road trips, temporary car insurance can cover the following:

  • Borrowing a car – Quick journey? Get an hour’s cover and be on your way.
  • Short-term cover – Student home for the holidays? Cover them for a month and only pay for the time they need.
  • Test drive time – Get a feel for the wheels. You can also drive home with temporary car insurance.
  • Share the drive – Long journey ahead? Take turns behind the wheel.
  • In an emergency – Get where you need to go in a matter of minutes.
  • Holiday driving – Cover your next road trip in the UK and abroad.
