Frequently asked questions

Helpful information about getting temporary car insurance online.

How do I get a temporary car insurance quote?

Enter your vehicle registration and choose a duration to begin. The quote page will guide you through the driver and vehicle details needed to continue.

How long can I get temporary car insurance for?

Available durations are shown during the quote process and depend on the options offered for your details.

How do I sign in to my customer portal?

Select Sign in at the top of this page. After entering your login details successfully, you will be redirected to your customer dashboard.

Where can I find my policy documents?

Sign in to your customer portal to view the policy information and documents available in your account.

When does my cover start?

Check the start date and time shown in your policy confirmation. Cover is subject to successful purchase, insurer acceptance and the terms of your policy.
