FAQs

Questions before you start?

Some quick information about using the TempCover website.

How do I get a quote?

Select “Get a quote” and follow the quote page to enter the vehicle and driver details requested.

What type of insurance is this website for?

This website is focused on temporary car insurance.

How do I access my customer portal?

Select “Sign in” at the top of the page. Enter your account details on the login page. After a successful login, you will be redirected to your customer dashboard.

Where can I view my policy information?

Sign in to your customer portal to view the policy information available in your account.

When does cover become active?

Please check the policy details and confirmation provided during your purchase. Cover is subject to eligibility, underwriting, acceptance and the terms of the policy.
